Alowish is a cloud-based AI platform that helps you understand the physical and mental health risks of traveling to any destination, at any time of year. Whether you're managing a chronic condition, traveling while pregnant, supporting an elderly family member, or simply want peace of mind before your next trip, Alowish gives you a science-backed, personalized risk picture — not generic travel advisories.

## Key Capabilities

**Personalized Risk Score** — Alowish combines your health profile with 343 parameters across 11 AI subnetworks (EBF-NN v3) to produce a 0–100 risk score and a confidence rating for your specific destination and travel dates.

**Real-Time RADAR** — The platform continuously ingests live weather feeds, Air Quality Index (AQI) readings, altitude data, seasonal disease calendars, and drug-environment alerts to keep your score current, not just at the time of booking.

**Smart Life Map** — An interactive GIS + AI map overlays color-coded route risk, nearby hospitals, pharmacies, and vetted safe-dining options so you can navigate your destination confidently.

**Dr. Alowish** — An AI chat assistant modeled on a conversational family doctor, available to answer follow-up health questions before and during your journey.

**Telemedicine Access** — Connect directly with specialist physicians for consultations before departure or while you're on the road.

**Partner API** — Travel agencies, insurers, and corporate travel platforms can embed Alowish risk intelligence directly into their products via a Swiss-hosted sovereign data API.
