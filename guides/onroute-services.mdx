Once you have plotted a route on the Alowish Life Map, a set of floating action buttons (FAB) along the map edge gives you instant access to five health services — all filtered and ranked specifically for your health profile. Whether you need a 24-hour specialist consultation, the nearest cardiac unit, or a restaurant that is safe for your food allergy, these services surface the right option at the right point in your journey.

Tap any FAB icon on the map to open that service panel. All panels can be opened in parallel — you do not need to close one before opening another.

---

## 🩺 Telemedicine

The Telemedicine service connects you to a qualified travel medicine specialist via live video call, available **24 hours a day, seven days a week**, from anywhere in the world with a data connection.

**When to use it:**

Use Telemedicine before a trip when your risk score is High or Very High and you want a specialist to review your profile, confirm your medication list, and co-design a mitigation plan. Use it during travel if you experience a symptom you are unsure about — chest tightness at altitude, a suspected allergic reaction, or a question about adjusting insulin for a new time zone. Use it in an emergency for immediate clinical triage while waiting for local emergency services to arrive.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Pre-Travel Consultation" icon="calendar-check">
    Review your Alowish risk score with a specialist, validate your carry-medication list, and receive a written travel health summary to carry with you.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Prescription Renewal" icon="file-prescription">
    Renew running prescriptions — including regular cardiac, diabetes, and respiratory medications — when you are abroad and unable to access your home GP.
  </Card>
  <Card title="INR Monitoring" icon="droplet">
    For patients on warfarin or other anticoagulants, discuss INR management during travel, including dietary changes at your destination and the nearest anticoagulation clinic.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Emergency Triage" icon="triangle-exclamation">
    Describe symptoms to a specialist in real time while awaiting local emergency services. The specialist can guide you through immediate first-aid steps and relay clinical information to receiving hospital staff.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Tip>
  Book a **pre-travel consultation** at least 72 hours before departure for non-emergency trips. This gives the specialist time to review your full Alowish profile and any supporting documents you upload, and gives you time to act on their recommendations before you leave.
</Tip>

## 🏥 Hospitals

The Hospitals panel lists medical facilities near your current map view, ranked not by distance alone but by **relevance to your health profile**. A cardiac patient will see hospitals with catheterisation labs and cardiac intensive care units ranked highest, even if a general district hospital is physically closer.

**When to use it:**

Use the Hospitals panel when you arrive at a destination or a waypoint on your route, so you always know where the nearest appropriate facility is before you need it urgently. Tap any hospital pin on the map to see its name, distance, key specialities, and estimated travel time.

<Accordion title="What the hospital listing shows">
  - **Hospital name and address**
  - **Distance** from your current map position
  - **Relevant specialities** matched to your profile (e.g., Cardiac Unit, ICU, Catheterisation Lab, Oncology, Renal Unit, Maternity)
  - **Emergency department status** (open 24/7 or limited hours)
  - **Languages spoken** (where data is available)
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Profile-matched speciality examples">
  | Your Condition | Prioritised Speciality |
  |---|---|
  | Heart disease / Hypertension | Cardiac unit, Cathlab, CCU |
  | Diabetes | Endocrinology, 24h pharmacy |
  | COPD / Asthma | Respiratory unit, Pulmonology |
  | Epilepsy / MS / Parkinson's | Neurology |
  | Renal disease | Nephrology, Dialysis unit |
  | Late pregnancy | Maternity, Obstetrics & Gynaecology |
  | Immunocompromised | Infectious disease, Oncology |
</Accordion>

<Note>
  Hospital data is sourced from national health registries, OpenStreetMap, and curated clinical databases. Data completeness varies by country. In regions with limited data coverage, the hospital panel will display a notice and recommend calling the local emergency number.
</Note>

## 🛡 Insurance

The Insurance panel surfaces **recommended travel insurance products** that match your clinical profile, filtered to ensure they cover your active conditions and the specific destination.

**When to use it:**

Use the Insurance panel when planning a trip, before finalising your booking. Standard travel insurance policies frequently exclude pre-existing conditions or impose sub-limits that make them inadequate for travellers with chronic illnesses. The Alowish Insurance panel filters out products that would not cover your profile and ranks those that would.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Condition-Inclusive Policies" icon="shield-check">
    Only policies that explicitly cover your declared conditions are shown. Policies with exclusions for your specific conditions are filtered out automatically.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Cardiac Rider Options" icon="heart">
    For cardiac profiles, the panel highlights policies offering cardiac riders — coverage extensions for cardiac events, emergency cardiac procedures, and repatriation following a cardiac admission.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Emergency Evacuation Cover" icon="helicopter">
    Policies that include medical evacuation and repatriation are flagged separately — particularly important for high-altitude destinations or countries with limited specialist care.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Coverage Verification" icon="circle-check">
    Tap any policy to see a plain-language summary of what is and is not covered for your profile before you click through to purchase.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Warning>
  Insurance recommendations shown in Alowish are informational and based on your self-reported profile. Always read the full policy documentation before purchasing. Ensure that all conditions are formally declared to the insurer at the point of application — failure to disclose a condition can invalidate a claim.
</Warning>

## 💊 Pharmacy

The Pharmacy panel locates the nearest open pharmacies along your route and flags them on the Life Map with a distinctive pin. It also presents a **key medications to carry checklist** derived from your Alowish profile and the recommendations generated for your destination.

**When to use it:**

Use the Pharmacy panel when you need to top up a medication supply during travel, source an over-the-counter product recommended in your Alowish report, or locate an after-hours pharmacy in an unfamiliar city. The checklist is particularly useful in the days before departure — work through it to confirm you have adequate supplies of every listed item before you leave home.

<Accordion title="Example carry checklist items by profile">
  | Condition / Factor | Checklist Item |
  |---|---|
  | Heart disease | GTN spray, aspirin, beta-blocker supply for full trip + 5 days extra |
  | Diabetes | Insulin (carry-on supply), testing strips, glucagon kit |
  | Severe allergy | EpiPen ×2, antihistamines, steroid tablets |
  | DVT / cardiac | Compression stockings (class 2), low-molecular-weight heparin if prescribed |
  | COPD / Asthma | Rescue inhaler, spacer, prednisolone emergency pack |
  | Warfarin | Anticoagulant card, INR monitoring kit or clinic list |
  | Malaria-risk destination | Antimalarial prophylaxis (confirm course length vs. trip duration) |
</Accordion>

<Tip>
  Carry **at least one week's extra supply** of all essential medications beyond your planned trip duration. Delays, lost luggage, and extended stays are common travel disruptions. Keep all medications in original labelled packaging and carry a doctor's letter listing each drug, its dose, and the reason for use — particularly important for controlled substances and injectable medications.
</Tip>

## 🍽 Safe Dining

The Safe Dining panel surfaces restaurant options along your route that have been filtered for compatibility with your health profile. Results are ranked by how well each venue's menu aligns with your active allergies and any dietary requirements generated by your conditions.

**When to use it:**

Use the Safe Dining panel whenever you are ready to eat during your journey and want to avoid the risk of an allergic reaction or an unsuitable meal for your condition. It is especially valuable in countries where you do not speak the language and cannot independently verify ingredients or preparation methods.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Allergen-Aware Filtering" icon="circle-exclamation">
    Restaurants that reliably accommodate your active allergens — including food allergies, nut-free kitchens, or shellfish-free menus — are ranked above those that cannot confirm safe preparation.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Heart-Healthy Options" icon="heart">
    For cardiac and hypertensive profiles, venues offering low-sodium, Mediterranean-style, or cardiac-friendly menus are flagged with a heart-healthy tag.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Diabetic-Friendly" icon="apple-whole">
    Venues with low-glycaemic-index menu options, accurate carbohydrate labelling, or dedicated diabetic menus are highlighted for diabetic profiles.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Renal Diet Compatible" icon="droplet">
    For renal disease profiles, venues that can accommodate low-potassium or low-phosphorus dietary requirements are surfaced where data is available.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Note>
  Safe Dining recommendations are based on publicly available menu data, venue-reported allergen information, and user-verified reports. Alowish cannot guarantee preparation methods in any kitchen. Always inform your server of your allergy or dietary requirement directly, even at a venue flagged as safe in the app.
</Note>
