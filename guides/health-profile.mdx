Your health profile is the foundation of every risk score Alowish produces. The more completely and accurately you fill it in, the more precisely the platform's AI engine — drawing on **343 clinical and environmental parameters** — can model the real health risks of any journey you plan.

<Note>
  Alowish evaluates your profile against **343 parameters** spanning clinical guidelines, environmental data, and travel medicine evidence. Each field you complete sharpens the accuracy of your risk score and the relevance of the recommendations you receive.
</Note>

## Open Your Profile

Navigate to the **Profile** section from the main navigation bar. You will find four tabs — **Conditions**, **Health**, **Allergy**, and **Vaccines** — each covering a distinct dimension of your health. Work through them in order.

---

<Steps>

  <Step title="Conditions Tab — Select Your Chronic Conditions">
    The Conditions tab presents a set of pill-shaped toggle buttons, one for each recognised condition. Tap or click any pill that applies to you to activate it; tap it again to deactivate.

    The available conditions are:

    | Category | Conditions |
    |---|---|
    | Cardiovascular | Heart disease, Hypertension, Previous DVT/PE |
    | Metabolic | Diabetes, Obesity |
    | Respiratory | COPD, Asthma |
    | Oncology | Cancer |
    | Neurological | MS, Parkinson's, Epilepsy |
    | Renal & Hepatic | Renal disease, Liver disease |
    | Immune | Immunocompromised |
    | Obstetric | Late pregnancy |

    <Warning>
      Each active condition increases your baseline risk score. Selecting conditions accurately is critical — underreporting will produce a score that understates your true travel risk.
    </Warning>

    You can select multiple conditions simultaneously. There is no limit to the number of active conditions in your profile.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Health Tab — Set BMI Range and Personal Factors">
    The Health tab captures two types of information: your **BMI range** and a set of **personal factor toggles** that provide the AI with important clinical and situational context.

    **BMI Range**

    Select the BMI bracket that best describes you from the dropdown or slider. Alowish uses BMI range — not exact weight — to protect your privacy while still informing relevant risk factors such as cardiovascular strain at altitude.

    **Personal Factors**

    Toggle each factor that applies to your upcoming trip:

    <CardGroup cols={2}>
      <Card title="Age 65+" icon="user-clock" href="/guides/reading-your-risk-score">
        Enables age-adjusted physiological modelling, including thermoregulation, immune response, and medication interaction risks.
      </Card>
      <Card title="Traveling Alone" icon="person-walking" href="/guides/reading-your-risk-score">
        Increases the weight given to emergency-access recommendations and telemedicine readiness in your report.
      </Card>
      <Card title="Mobility Impairment" icon="wheelchair" href="/guides/onroute-services">
        Flags accessibility requirements and surfaces accessible hospital and pharmacy pins on the Life Map.
      </Card>
      <Card title="Previous Adverse Travel Event" icon="triangle-exclamation" href="/guides/reading-your-risk-score">
        Signals heightened personal sensitivity; the AI applies a conservative risk margin to its scoring.
      </Card>
      <Card title="Medical Professional" icon="stethoscope" href="/guides/reading-your-risk-score">
        Unlocks additional clinical detail in recommendations, including drug dosing notes and differential risk breakdowns.
      </Card>
      <Card title="Comprehensive Insurance" icon="shield-check" href="/guides/onroute-services">
        Adjusts insurance recommendations shown in the Life Map services panel and reduces the insurance-gap risk sub-score.
      </Card>
    </CardGroup>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Allergy Tab — Select Active Allergies">
    The Allergy tab lists every supported allergen category as pill buttons. Activate each allergy that currently applies to you — not allergies you have grown out of or that are fully controlled.

    <Tabs>
      <Tab title="Food & Insect">
        - Food allergies (general)
        - Insect sting (bee, wasp, hornet)
      </Tab>
      <Tab title="Drug & Chemical">
        - Penicillin
        - NSAIDs (e.g., ibuprofen, aspirin)
        - Latex
      </Tab>
      <Tab title="Environmental">
        - Photosensitivity
        - Hayfever (grass/tree pollen)
        - Mould spores
        - Dust mite
        - Animal dander
      </Tab>
    </Tabs>

    Each active allergy feeds directly into destination-specific risk modelling. For example, activating **Hayfever** combined with a spring trip to a high-pollen city will raise the Allergy sub-score and trigger specific antihistamine carry recommendations.

    <Tip>
      If you carry an **EpiPen** or other emergency allergy medication, ensure you have also entered it in the **Medications** field so Alowish can confirm it in your carry checklist recommendations.
    </Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Vaccines Tab — Record Your Vaccination History">
    The Vaccines tab lets you mark every vaccination or prophylactic medication you have received. Unlike conditions and allergies, **each vaccine you record reduces your overall risk score** by removing a category of preventable illness from your exposure profile.

    Select all vaccines that apply:

    <Accordion title="Routine & Boosters">
      - Influenza (Flu)
      - COVID-19
      - MMR (Measles, Mumps, Rubella)
      - Varicella (Chickenpox)
      - Tetanus
      - Pneumococcal
      - Polio
    </Accordion>

    <Accordion title="Travel-Specific">
      - Hepatitis A
      - Hepatitis B
      - Typhoid
      - Yellow fever
      - Rabies
      - Meningococcal
      - Japanese Encephalitis
      - Cholera / ETEC
      - Tick-borne encephalitis
    </Accordion>

    <Accordion title="Prophylaxis">
      - Malaria prophylaxis (indicate if currently taking a course)
    </Accordion>

    <Note>
      Alowish does not store vaccine dates. If a vaccine you received is no longer within its effective window for your destination, the platform will flag this in the **Active Risk Factors** list on your score card and suggest a booster consultation.
    </Note>
  </Step>

</Steps>

## How Your Profile Affects Your Score

The table below summarises the directional impact each profile section has on your risk score:

| Profile Section | Effect on Score | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Chronic conditions | ↑ Increases | Each condition adds clinical vulnerability parameters |
| BMI range (elevated) | ↑ Increases | Higher physiological load at altitude, heat, and exertion |
| Personal factors | ↑ or ↓ | Depends on factor (e.g., insurance lowers; solo travel raises) |
| Active allergies | ↑ Increases | Environmental exposure risk at destination |
| Vaccines recorded | ↓ Decreases | Preventable-disease risk removed from model |

## Privacy and Data Security

Your health profile is stored exclusively on **Swiss-based cloud infrastructure**, subject to Swiss data protection law and GDPR. Profile data is encrypted at rest and in transit, and is never sold or shared with third parties. Only you — and any clinician you explicitly invite — can access your profile.

<Tip>
  Review and update your profile **before every new trip**, even if your health has not changed significantly. Seasonal factors, new medications, and recently administered vaccines can all shift your risk score materially. A profile that was accurate for a winter city break may understate risk for a summer trek at altitude.
</Tip>
