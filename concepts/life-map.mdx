The Life Map is Alowish's interactive geographic intelligence layer — a GIS-powered smart map that translates your risk score from an abstract number into a visual journey you can read at a glance. Every road segment, every waypoint, and every stop is colored according to the health risk it represents for *you specifically*, based on your active health verticals, your current medications, and live environmental data for that precise location and time. The Life Map updates continuously as you travel, so the picture you see always reflects current conditions rather than a static pre-departure snapshot.

## Color-Coded Route Lines

Route lines on the Life Map are segmented and colored in real time to show how your health risk changes along the way — not just at the destination.

| Color | Risk Band | What It Means |
|---|---|---|
| 🟢 **Green** | Low (< 30 %) | This segment presents low environmental stress for your profile. Proceed with normal precautions. |
| 🟠 **Orange** | Moderate (30 – 64 %) | Elevated risk on this segment — review the flagged parameters before driving or passing through. |
| 🔴 **Red** | High / Very High (≥ 65 %) | Significant health risk on this segment. Consider an alternative route or timing, or consult a specialist. |

Segments turn red for reasons specific to your profile: a mountain pass may be green for a healthy traveler but red for someone on beta-blockers, because altitude blunts the cardiac response that altitude-sickness early-warning systems depend on.

## Route Comparison Cards

At the bottom of the Life Map screen, Alowish presents side-by-side **Route Comparison Cards** — one for each available route type. Each card shows:

- **Route name** (Train, Motorway, Scenic)
- **Overall risk score** for the full journey on that route, color-coded to its band
- **Estimated travel time**
- **Key risk factors** specific to that route (e.g., *"Altitude gain: 1,200 m"* for Scenic, or *"Air-conditioned carriage reduces heat stress"* for Train)

<Steps>
  <Step title="View route cards">
    Tap the **Routes** button in the bottom toolbar to expand the comparison tray. All available route options appear as swipeable cards.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Switch your active route">
    Tap any route card to make it your active route. The map redraws instantly with that route's colored segments and updates your overall risk score in the top panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review risk details">
    Tap the **Details** chevron on a route card to expand a full breakdown — each segment score, the environmental factors driving it, and Alowish's recommendation for that stretch.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Train routes consistently score lower for cardiac and respiratory travelers because they eliminate exertion, provide climate control, and run through populated corridors with hospital access. Always check the Train card first if one is available.
</Tip>

## Journey Timeline Bar

A horizontal timeline bar runs along the bottom edge of the map, above the route cards. It represents your entire journey from departure to arrival, colored segment by segment to show how your risk profile evolves over time and distance. Drag the scrubber left or right to preview risk conditions at any point in your journey — useful for planning rest stops, medication timing, or deciding where to overnight.

Five Floating Action Buttons sit in a vertical stack on the right edge of the Life Map, giving you one-tap access to the services you are most likely to need on the road.

## Interacting With the Map

- **Tap a colored route segment** to see a tooltip with the specific environmental parameters driving the risk color at that point (e.g., AQI 142, PM2.5 elevated, temperature 38 °C).
- **Tap any map pin** to open its popup. Hospital popups include specialty tags relevant to your verticals. Pharmacy popups highlight medication compatibility. Restaurant popups flag allergy risks.
- **Pinch to zoom** — the map reloads pin density and segment detail at each zoom level, so you get street-level granularity when you need it and a regional overview when planning ahead.
- **Long-press the map** to drop a custom waypoint and get an instant risk score for that specific location given current conditions.

<Note>
  Life Map data refreshes every 60 seconds when you are actively traveling. In areas with limited connectivity, Alowish caches the last known risk layer and displays a banner indicating the age of the cached data so you are never unaware of a stale reading.
</Note>
