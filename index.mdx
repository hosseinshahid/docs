## Get up and running

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Sign up at [app.alowish.com](https://app.alowish.com/signup). No credit card required for the free tier.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build your health profile">
    Enter your conditions, medications, allergies, and vaccination history. Your profile is encrypted and stored on our Swiss sovereign data infrastructure.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Search your destination">
    Type any city or place into the search bar. Alowish fetches live environmental data — air quality, temperature, altitude, and more — and calculates your personalized risk score instantly.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Follow your Life Map">
    View your color-coded route risk, compare travel options, and tap any hospital, pharmacy, or safe restaurant pin for details. Travel confidently with real-time alerts along the way.
  </Step>
</Steps>
